On a Friday night in San Francisco, Leo and his classmates walked into **Mozzeria**, a famous pizza restaurant. As they entered, the delicious smell of wood-fired dough filled the air. But what made Mozzeria unique was that it was entirely Deaf-owned and Deaf-staffed. 

When the waiter, Luis, arrived at their table, he smiled and held out his hands. Leo decided to try ordering in ASL. 

Leo signed: `I HUNGRY` (running his curved C-handshape down the center of his chest). 

Luis nodded and signed: `DRINK WHAT?` (tilting a curved C-handshape toward his mouth).

## 2. Eating and Drinking Signs

Many food signs are highly iconic, resembling the physical action of eating or drinking.

* **Food/Eat**: Flat-O hand tapping lips twice (for verb **EAT**, make a single, larger movement).
* **Drink**: C-hand shape tilting toward mouth.
* **Hungry**: Curved C-hand sliding down chest.
* **Thirsty**: Index finger tracing down throat.

    **Question 1**: How do the signs for 'Food' and 'Eat' differ?
    
    *Answer*: 'Food' (noun) uses a small, repeating double tap of the flat-O hand to the mouth. 'Eat' (verb) uses a single, larger movement toward the mouth.

    *Answer*: The curved "C" handshape sliding down the chest represents the physical path of food traveling down the throat.

## 3. Likes and Dislikes

Expressing preferences uses chest-anchored movements and facial expressions.

* **Favorite**: Middle finger tapping chin twice.
* **Like**: Pulling middle finger/thumb from chest (pinching).
* **Don't Like**: Performing 'Like' then twisting palm out and throwing hand down.
