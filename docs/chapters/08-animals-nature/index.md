## 1. Walk in Muir Woods: Signs of the Wild

On a quiet Sunday morning, Liam and his mother walked along the dirt trails of Muir Woods, surrounded by towering redwood trees. As they walked, Liam's mother pointed up to the branches. 

`LOOK`, she signed. `BIRD` (tapping her index finger and thumb together near her mouth like a beak). 

Liam looked up and saw a blue jay perched on a branch. Just then, a rustle in the bushes caught their attention. A small cat-like shape slipped through the ferns. 

Liam signed: `CAT?` (drawing "F" handshapes outward from his cheeks to mimic whiskers). 

His mother shook her head. `NO. DOG` (tapping her thigh and snapping her fingers to mimic calling a pet). As they watched, a stray dog ran down the path, its owner running behind it. Liam realized that animal signs in ASL were like drawings in the air—each sign captured a physical characteristic of the animal, making them easy to identify and remember.

## 2. Animal Vocabulary

Animal signs are highly iconic, mimicking physical traits.

* **Dog**: Thigh tap followed by finger snap.
* **Cat**: Drawing "F" whiskers outward from cheeks.
* **Bird**: Tapping beak near mouth.
* **Animal**: Shoulder roll with curved fingertips resting on chest.

??? question "Check Your Understanding - Click to Expand"
    **Question 1**: Why is the sign for 'Cat' signed with an 'F' handshape?
    
    *Answer*: The circle of the 'F' handshape represents the whisker base, and pulling it outward mimics the length of a cat's whiskers.
    
    **Question 2**: What is the movement for the sign 'Animal'?
    
    *Answer*: Place curved hands on the chest and roll the shoulders forward twice (mimicking breathing or ribcage movement).
