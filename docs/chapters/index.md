# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 12 chapters covering 200 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Introduction and ASL Foundations](01-intro-asl-foundations/index.md) - Introduces the basic parameters of American Sign Language (handshape, palm orientation, location, movement, expression) and Deaf culture basics.
2. [Fingerspelling and Numbers](02-fingerspelling-numbers/index.md) - Teaches fingerspelling the ASL alphabet, numbers 1-100, counting techniques, and basic time and age expressions.
3. [Basic Conversational Phrases](03-basic-conversation/index.md) - Covers essential signs for greetings, politeness, simple questions, and basic everyday dialogue.
4. [Pronouns and Signing Space](04-pronouns-signing-space/index.md) - Explores how to use personal pronouns, indexing, directionality, and the physical signing space to structure communication.
5. [Expressing Emotions and Feelings](05-emotions-feelings/index.md) - Enables students to communicate different feelings, emotions, states of being, and varying degrees of emotional intensity.
6. [Family and People](06-family-people/index.md) - Introduces signs for family members, friends, relationships, and different roles in school and the community.
7. [Food and Favorite Things](07-food-favorites/index.md) - Focuses on food vocabulary, expressing likes/dislikes, ordering meals, and describing preferences.
8. [Animals and Nature](08-animals-nature/index.md) - Covers signs for pets, common wild animals, plants, weather, and the natural environment.
9. [Describing the World](09-describing-world/index.md) - Introduces descriptive words including colors, size, speed, temperature, and general qualities.
10. [Action Verbs and Activities](10-action-verbs-activities/index.md) - Teaches common action signs for play, sports, school work, daily routines, and performances.
11. [ASL Grammar and Expression](11-asl-grammar-expression/index.md) - Explores grammatical structures like role shifts, classifiers, agreement verbs, and non-manual signals (facial grammar).
12. [Deaf Culture and Communication](12-deaf-culture-communication/index.md) - Examines Deaf identity, history, communication accessibility, regional sign variations, and visual storytelling.

## How to Use This Textbook

This textbook uses a step-by-step learning progression. Each chapter builds upon the concepts introduced in the previous chapters, ensuring that all prerequisites are met before advanced topics are introduced. We recommend completing the chapters in sequence.

**Note:** Each chapter includes a list of concepts covered. Make sure to complete prerequisites before moving to advanced chapters.
